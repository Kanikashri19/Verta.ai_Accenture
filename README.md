# Verta.ai — Business Intelligence, Reimagined

> **From KPI movement → to root cause → to evidence → to action.**

Verta.ai is a governed **KPI intelligence-to-action engine** that helps businesses understand what changed, why it changed, how confident they should be, and what action to take next.



**Architecture**

```text
Enterprise Data
      ↓
KPI Semantic Layer
      ↓
Anomaly & Statistical Analysis
      ↓
Driver Decomposition
      ↓
Evidence / RAG
      ↓
Confidence & Governance
      ↓
 ┌───────────────┐
 │               │
PROCEED       ABSTAIN
 │               │
 ↓               ↓
LLM          Human Review
 │
 ↓
Persona-Specific Insight
      ↓
Recommended Actions

```

**Key Features**
1. KPI Intelligence
2. Driver Analysis
3. Evidence-Grounded RAG
4. Confidence & Governance
5. Persona Intelligence
6. Action Recommendations
